    How results are checked

    Every device's first results are rechecked by rerunning them on another idle device, then a random share after that. A wrong result suspends the device without pay and the task is rerun elsewhere. Rechecking more catches cheats sooner but costs more; try the Lean verification scenario to see bad results slip through.

    Reliability score: share of checks passed, weighted by how often the device finishes tasks without being interrupted.

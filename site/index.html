Read the film transcript
  1. By day, a laptop is in use from morning until 11pm. Your laptop works hard all day. Then it sits idle for hours.
  2. At night its screen switches to Idle, with the status tags On, Plugged in and Idle. Nightshift measures the quiet hours. Only device state: on, plugged in, idle.
  3. A dashboard, labelled sample data, counts up to 5.8 hours of usable idle today and 46 core-hours this week. See what your idle time adds up to.
  4. Icons for files, browsing and keystrokes are crossed out. Private by design. Never your files, your browsing or your keystrokes.
  5. A batch job splits into small pieces that travel to idle laptops, marked Planned. Next: matching idle laptops with teams that need compute.
  6. End card: Nightshift. Join the 30-day pilot. S$20 pilot payment, pause any time, device state only.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Email us.

Will it slow my laptop?

The app is designed to use under 1% of your CPU, and runs no jobs during the pilot.

Can I earn money?

The pilot pays a flat amount for 30 days. Earnings from real jobs come in a later phase, and figures before then are estimates.

Which laptops can join?

Personal Windows or Mac laptops with admin rights and at least 8 GB memory. Managed, shared, virtual and tablet devices are not eligible.

Who can see my data?

Only the project team, under a consent form you can review. You can export or delete your data any time.

Will it use more electricity?

A little, while the laptop stays awake. The dashboard estimates the cost.

What about battery wear and heat?

The app is being designed to work only while plugged in, and to pause when the laptop is busy or hot. The pilot runs no compute jobs, so it adds almost no load.

How do payouts work?

The pilot payment is sent after 30 days by PayNow or bank transfer. Later, job earnings would sit in a wallet and be paid out through a licensed payment provider, with a minimum withdrawal.

How do I stop?

Pause or uninstall the app any time, and ask us to delete your data. Nothing is left running.

Who are the buyers?

Teams with batch work that has no sensitive data, such as rendering, data processing and research simulations. We are still finding our first design partners.

Privacy

Privacy notice and consent

How we handle pilot data, in plain words.

What we collect and why

Whether your laptop is on, plugged in and idle, CPU, GPU and memory load, hardware specs, network type and simple security checks. We use it only to measure idle capacity during the pilot.

What we never collect

Your files, browsing history, keystrokes, screen contents or location.

How long we keep it and where

Kept for the pilot and 12 months after, then deleted or anonymised. Stored in Singapore by Supabase.

Website analytics

This website counts page visits and button clicks with PostHog to see which pages help people decide. It sets no cookies, keeps its identifier only until you close the tab, and does not record your keystrokes or what you type into forms.

Your rights

You can withdraw consent, request access to your data, ask for corrections or deletion, and uninstall at any time by contacting us.

Who can take part

People aged 18 or over, on a personal laptop they own, with admin rights.
